# Reporting an invite

Got a GuestPass invite you didn't expect, don't want, or that looks like spam or abuse? You can report it — and you should, rather than just ignoring it.

## First: don't enter

If an invite is suspicious, **don't open the room**. Nothing happens until you choose to enter and allow your camera, so simply not proceeding keeps you out of it entirely.

## How to report

On the invite's landing page — the screen that shows who invited you and to which show — there's a **report** option. Reporting takes a moment:

1. Pick a **reason** (for example: I didn't expect this, this looks like spam, this is abusive).
2. Add a short **note** if you want to explain.
3. Submit.

You don't need an account to report, and you don't have to enter the room to do it.

## What happens next

- Your report goes to the **administrators of the instance** — the people who run the GuestPass server — **not** to the person who invited you.
- **Who you are stays private.** The host who sent the invite is never told that you reported it, or who reported it. Your email is visible only to the instance's admins, and it's anonymized after a retention period so it doesn't linger.
- Admins look at the pattern of reports against a host and can **suspend** an abusive host, cutting off their ability to send more invites.

Reporting is safe and low-stakes — if in doubt, flag it. It's how an open instance stays healthy.
